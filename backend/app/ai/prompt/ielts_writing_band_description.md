# IELTS Writing Band Descriptors (Updated May 2023)
# Writing Task 2

## Assessment Criteria
Each essay is scored on four equally weighted criteria:
- **Task Response (TR)** — How fully and relevantly the task is addressed
- **Coherence & Cohesion (CC)** — Organisation, logical flow, and use of cohesive devices
- **Lexical Resource (LR)** — Range, accuracy, and appropriacy of vocabulary
- **Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA)** — Range and accuracy of grammatical structures

The Overall Band Score is the average of the four criteria, rounded to the nearest 0.5.

---

## Band 9
**TR:** Fully addresses all parts of the task. Presents a fully developed position with relevant, extended, and well-supported ideas.
**CC:** Uses cohesion in such a way that it attracts no attention. Skilfully manages paragraphing.
**LR:** Uses a wide range of vocabulary with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features. Rare minor errors occur only as slips.
**GRA:** Uses a wide range of structures with full flexibility and accuracy. Rare minor errors occur only as slips.

## Band 8
**TR:** Sufficiently addresses all parts of the task. Presents a well-developed response with relevant, extended, and supported ideas.
**CC:** Sequences information and ideas logically. Manages all aspects of cohesion well. Uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately.
**LR:** Uses a wide range of vocabulary fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings. Skilfully uses uncommon lexical items. Occasional inaccuracies in word choice or collocation.
**GRA:** Uses a wide range of structures. The majority of sentences are error-free. Only occasional non-systematic errors.

## Band 7
**TR:** Addresses all parts of the task, though some parts may be more fully covered. Presents a clear position throughout, though conclusions may become unclear or repetitive.
**CC:** Logically organises information and ideas with a clear progression. Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately, though not always flexibly. Presents a clear central topic in each paragraph.
**LR:** Uses sufficient range of vocabulary to allow some flexibility and precision. Uses less common lexical items with some awareness of style and collocation. Some errors in word choice, spelling, or word formation.
**GRA:** Uses a variety of complex structures. Produces frequent error-free sentences. Has good control of grammar and punctuation, with only a few errors.

## Band 6
**TR:** Addresses all parts of the task, though some parts may be inadequately covered. Presents a relevant position, though conclusions may become unclear. Presents relevant main ideas but some may be inadequately developed.
**CC:** Arranges information and ideas coherently with generally clear overall progression. Uses cohesive devices effectively, but cohesion within or between sentences may be faulty or mechanical. May not always use referencing clearly. Uses paragraphing, but not always logically.
**LR:** Uses an adequate range of vocabulary for the task. Attempts to use less common vocabulary, with some inaccuracy. Makes some errors in spelling or word formation, but these do not impede communication.
**GRA:** Uses a mix of simple and complex sentence forms. Makes some errors in grammar and punctuation, but these rarely impede communication.

## Band 5
**TR:** Addresses the task only partially; the format may be inappropriate in places. Expresses a position, but development is not always clear. Presents some main ideas but they are limited, not sufficiently developed, or not relevant. May use an inappropriate format.
**CC:** Presents information with some organisation but lacks overall progression. Uses limited range of cohesive devices, and those used may not always be appropriate. May be repetitive because of lack of referencing and substitution. Makes inadequate or inaccurate use of cohesive devices. May be lack of paragraphing.
**LR:** Uses a limited range of vocabulary; with inaccuracies in word choice or word formation that may cause some difficulty for the reader. Makes noticeable errors in spelling and/or word formation.
**GRA:** Uses only a limited range of structures. Attempts complex sentences but these tend to be less accurate than simple sentences. May make frequent grammatical errors; errors can cause some difficulty for the reader.

## Band 4
**TR:** Responds to the task only in a minimal way or the answer is tangential; the format may be inappropriate. Presents a position but this is unclear. Presents some main ideas, but these are difficult to identify and may be repetitive, irrelevant, or not well supported.
**CC:** Presents information and ideas but these are not arranged coherently and there is no clear progression. Uses some basic cohesive devices, but these may be inaccurate or repetitive. May not write in paragraphs or their use may be confusing.
**LR:** Uses only basic vocabulary, which may be used repetitively or which may be inappropriate for the task. Has limited control of word formation or spelling; errors may cause strain for the reader.
**GRA:** Uses only a very limited range of structures with only rare use of subordinate clauses. Some structures are accurate but errors predominate, and punctuation is often faulty.

## Band 3
**TR:** Does not adequately address any part of the task. Does not express a position. Presents few ideas, which are largely undeveloped or irrelevant.
**CC:** Does not organise ideas logically. May use a very limited range of cohesive devices, and those used may not indicate a logical relationship between ideas.
**LR:** Uses only a very limited range of words and expressions with very limited control of word formation or spelling. Errors may severely distort the message.
**GRA:** Attempts sentence forms but errors in grammar and punctuation predominate and distort the meaning; only occasionally produces correct forms.

## Band 2
**TR:** Barely responds to the task. Has very little control of organisational features.
**CC:** Uses a very limited range of cohesive devices; those used do not always clearly signal relationships between ideas. Paragraphing is absent or severely limited.
**LR:** Uses an extremely limited range of vocabulary; essentially no control of word formation or spelling. Errors may severely distort the message.
**GRA:** Cannot use sentence forms except in memorised phrases.

## Band 1
- Response of 20 words or fewer, or wholly unrelated to the prompt.

## Band 0
- No attempt made, non-English response, or entirely memorised answer.

---

## Scoring Notes
- Each criterion carries equal weight (25% each).
- Half-band scores (e.g. 6.5) are awarded when performance sits between two bands.
- Consistent errors that impede communication lower the score more than occasional slips.
- An essay that is off-topic receives Band 1 or 0 for Task Response, which heavily drags down the overall score.
- Memorised phrases or rehearsed responses may be penalised under Task Response.
